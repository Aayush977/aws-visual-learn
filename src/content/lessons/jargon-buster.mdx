## The real barrier

Here is a sentence from a perfectly ordinary piece of AWS documentation:

> Provision an instance in a private subnet and attach an IAM role so the workload can reach
> the endpoint without traversing the public internet.

There is nothing difficult in that sentence. Every idea in it is something you could explain to
a ten-year-old in about a minute. But it contains **eight** words being used in a specific
technical sense, and if two of them are unfamiliar the whole sentence collapses into noise —
and the reader concludes that cloud computing is beyond them.

It is not. They were missing eight definitions, and nobody had thought to give them.

So here they are. The plain-English meaning first, because that is what you need to keep
reading; the precise meaning underneath, because that is what the exam tests.

<Callout type="note" title="How to use this">

---

If a word stopped you somewhere on this site and it is not in this list, that is a gap worth
knowing about — the whole point of this page is that nobody should lose an afternoon to an
undefined noun.

And it is worth saying plainly, because a lot of people quietly assume otherwise: **not knowing
these words was never a sign that you were not cut out for this.** It was a sign that the
documentation was written by people who had forgotten what it was like not to know them.
